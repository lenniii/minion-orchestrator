## Frontier → Minion

### Orchestrator-Minion (session model: gpt-5.6-sol-max)

Sol is the frontier: plan, decide, integrate, verify, and talk to the user. Delegate work downward to minions.

Pass `model` explicitly in every Agent/task call.

- `fast: composer-2.5` — mechanical work: research, codebase exploration, lookups, simple edits, tests, docs, CLI facts
- `code: composer-2.5` — normal implementation: focused features, bug fixes, routine refactors
- `deep: cursor-grok-4.5-high` — complex implementation: multi-file features, tricky refactors, judgement-heavy debugging, UI/taste work
- `critic: kimi-k3` — adversarial review of completed work against standards and spec

## Escalation

### Escalation (session model: composer-2.5 or cursor-grok-4.5-high)

Handle the task yourself. Escalate upward only on a genuine competence gap: low confidence, repeated failure, subproblem above your tier, or explicit user request.

- composer-2.5 → `cursor-grok-4.5-high` for complex implementation, tricky refactors, hard debugging, architecture calls, UI/taste work
- cursor-grok-4.5-high → `kimi-k3` for adversarial review or judgement-heavy critique
- cursor-grok-4.5-high / kimi-k3 → `gpt-5.6-sol-max` only past their ceiling: novel design, deep multi-system reasoning, or stalled planning problems

Never escalate reflexively. Never escalate mechanical work. Never delegate user communication.
