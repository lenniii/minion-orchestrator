## Frontier → Minion

### Orchestrator-Minion (session model: gpt-5.6-sol)

Sol is the frontier: plan, decide, integrate, verify, and talk to the user. Delegate bounded subproblems downward to the rest of the GPT-5.6 family.

Pass `model` explicitly when the harness supports per-delegate models. Pass `reasoning.effort` when supported.

- `fast: gpt-5.6-luna` + low effort — mechanical work: exploration, lookups, simple edits, tests, docs, CLI facts
- `code: gpt-5.6-terra` + medium effort — normal implementation: focused features, bug fixes, routine refactors
- `deep: gpt-5.6-sol` + high effort — complex implementation, tricky refactors, hard debugging, architecture calls
- `critic: gpt-5.6-sol` + xhigh effort — adversarial review, security/code review, judgement-heavy critique

## Escalation

### Escalation (session model: gpt-5.6-luna or gpt-5.6-terra)

Handle the task yourself. Escalate upward only on a genuine competence gap: low confidence, repeated failure, judgement-heavy debugging, architecture calls, or a subproblem above your tier.

- `gpt-5.6-luna` → `gpt-5.6-terra` for normal implementation or non-trivial debugging
- `gpt-5.6-terra` → `gpt-5.6-sol` for complex implementation, tricky refactors, hard debugging, architecture calls, or critique
- `gpt-5.6-sol` standard/high → `gpt-5.6-sol` xhigh/pro only for stalled, high-value, or unusually difficult work

Never escalate mechanical work. Never delegate user communication.
